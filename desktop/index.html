Tracking counts clicks, key presses, and scroll events across applications. Mouse movement marks active time. It never saves your words, key values, mouse positions, screenshots, or app names.

After 60 seconds without input, time is counted as idle. Sleep and screen lock pause tracking. Tracking always starts paused when you open the app.

While tracking, closing this window keeps it running in the system tray. Use the tray to pause or quit. Your saved hourly counts sync automatically about once a minute.

On this computer, you will need to reconnect when the app restarts.

Activity describes computer use. It does not measure productivity, hydration, or health.
